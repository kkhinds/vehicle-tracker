Fuel price fixes, and the version is now visible in the app.

**The price field opens at today's price.** It used to carry over whatever your last fill-up cost — $3.26, when diesel has actually been $3.21 since May. It now opens at the current published price for that vehicle's fuel. Type over it and your figure stays; nothing overwrites what you enter.

**Where the price comes from.** The app now reads the Barbados Government Information Service, which announces prices the day they take effect, so the line under the field reads "Barbados diesel is $3.21/L since 17 May 2026" instead of showing the date some website happened to look. globalpetrolprices.com is still the fallback.

**You can see it's live.** Under the price: who published it and when the app last checked. A price that hasn't moved in months no longer looks like an app that stopped checking.

**Use $3.21.** One tap drops the published price into the field, and the total works itself out from the litres.

**App version on screen.** Settings and Help now show which version you're running.

## Install

Download `Vehicle-Tracker-Setup-3.4.3.exe` and run it. Existing installs update themselves a few seconds after launch and install on quit.
