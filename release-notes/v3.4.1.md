Chart and readability fixes.

- **The monthly spend chart was lying.** Every month over about 85% of the biggest one drew at exactly the same height, so April at $3,604 and June at $4,098 looked identical. The bars now match the numbers.
- The two colours in that chart — fuel versus everything else — were so close you couldn't tell the split apart. They're properly separated now.
- Two bits of text on tinted backgrounds were too faint to read comfortably in dark mode: the note explaining a suspect fuel figure, and the ACTIVE tag in the garage.
- The "I missed logging fill-ups" and "Doesn't expire" checkboxes were tiny. They're a proper size now.

## Install

Download `Vehicle-Tracker-Setup-3.4.1.exe` and run it. Existing installs update themselves a few seconds after launch and install on quit.
