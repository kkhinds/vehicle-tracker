Expiry dates are visible now, not just wired to notifications.

- A document row says when it expires and how long is left: "registration · expires 3 May 2027 — 276 days left". Once past, "expired 12 days ago".
- An insurance row shows the renewal date and the countdown the same way.
- Opening either shows the date and days left, in red inside the last 30 days.
- Documents and policies stay on the road ahead for a full year rather than 240 days, so an annual renewal is visible the whole time instead of appearing eight months in.
- A document saved with no expiry date says so, and tells you that adding one turns on the warnings at 60, 30 and 7 days.

The reminders themselves already worked. What was missing was the app telling you anything while you were looking at it.

## Install

Download `Vehicle-Tracker-Setup-3.3.1.exe` and run it. Existing installs update themselves a few seconds after launch and install on quit.
