Small fixes on top of 3.1.0, and releases are now built automatically.

- Switching to miles or MPG now changes what the timeline shows. Fuel rows said "km/L" and the road ahead said "km" regardless of the setting. Service reminders and the tire warning had the same problem.
- An overdue service with no date attached floated to the top of the road ahead instead of sitting next to TODAY.
- The alert dots on the vehicle switcher are worked out in one pass now. The old way made each vehicle current in turn to ask about it, which wrote the database once per vehicle every time the view refreshed.

## Install

Download `Vehicle-Tracker-Setup-3.1.1.exe` and run it. Existing installs update themselves a few seconds after launch and install on quit.
