Logging a service now resets its interval.

**One entry instead of two.** Type what was done, say "Oil and filter, cabin air filter", and the form lists the service intervals it covers, already ticked. Save, and they're marked done at that odometer reading. Before this, the road ahead kept calling the oil change overdue until you went into Intervals and marked it done a second time.

One visit usually covers several jobs, so every interval it names is picked up, not just the first. Untick any that don't apply. Intervals you added yourself match by name. Editing an old service leaves the intervals alone.

**Services have a category again.** The form asks for one (Oil Change, Brake Service, Battery and so on), with suggestions as you type. Every service used to be saved as "Other". The category shows on the timeline and in the CSV export, and it fills in the interval when the description doesn't name one: "Tyre Rotation" with "did all four" still resets the rotation.

**Notes on every record.** Every log form has a notes box now: fill-ups, services, top-ups, tire checks, policies and documents. The app always had somewhere to store notes but never gave you a box to type in. Open a record and the notes show under its details, and search finds them. Enter starts a new line in the box. Ctrl+Enter saves.

**Odometer when bought.** The garage form has a field for the reading when you bought the vehicle. Cost per km counts from there, so a vehicle bought used isn't credited with distance someone else drove. Saving a vehicle also used to wipe this figure, so if your cost per km looked suspiciously low, enter it once in Garage › Edit.

## Install

Download `Vehicle-Tracker-Setup-3.4.5.exe` and run it. Existing installs update themselves a few seconds after launch and install on quit.
