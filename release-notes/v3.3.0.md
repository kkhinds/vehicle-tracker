## Fuel

**Price per litre works itself out.** Enter the litres and what you actually paid, and the app does the division. Enter a price per litre instead and the total follows. Whichever you typed last wins, and changing the litres recalculates the other one. No more doing pump maths in your head.

**Missed a fill-up? Say so.** Skipping a couple of fill-ups and then logging the next one at your current odometer used to double your economy figure — the distance covered fuel that was never recorded, but only the litres on record went into the sum. The fuel form has a "I missed logging fill-ups before this one" box now. A marked entry gets no economy figure and starts the next measurement fresh, so one gap stops poisoning the average. Tick it on an old entry by editing it.

**A nudge when a tank looks too good.** If a saved fill-up comes out far better than the vehicle's usual, the app suggests you probably missed one.

**Pump price cross-check.** About once a week the app reads the national petrol and diesel price for your country from globalpetrolprices.com and shows it under the price field. If what you entered is more than 20% off, it says so — usually a slipped decimal. It's a comparison only; your entry always wins. Settings has the cached figures and a Check now button.

## Also

- The dev build is branded — green icon, "DEV" in the window title and lens bar, its own taskbar button — so it can't be confused with the installed app.
- Help now states exactly what the app fetches (update check, pump price) and that neither sends any of your data.

## Install

Download `Vehicle-Tracker-Setup-3.3.0.exe` and run it. Existing installs update themselves a few seconds after launch and install on quit.
