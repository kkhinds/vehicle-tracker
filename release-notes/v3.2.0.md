More on the stats view, and the backups sheet finally shows what's on disk.

## What's new

**Fuel economy chart.** The stats view plots your last twelve full tanks, with best and latest next to it. Partial fills are left out — they don't close a measured span, so they have no figure of their own.

**Date range on stats.** 12 months, this year, or all time. It drives the category breakdown, the quarter and year rows, and the CSV export. The monthly bars stay at six months, because that's what that chart is.

**Backup snapshots are listed.** The sheet used to say "10 on disk" and leave it there. Now it lists them — newest first, with size and timestamp — and each one can be restored or deleted.

## Smaller

- The odometer used to count up from zero every time it changed, so logging a fill-up replayed the whole climb. It counts from the previous reading now.
- Search says when there are more matches than the forty it shows.

## Install

Download `Vehicle-Tracker-Setup-3.2.0.exe` and run it. Existing installs update themselves a few seconds after launch and install on quit.
