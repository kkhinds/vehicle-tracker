Fuel economy that tells the truth, and a fix for a setting that made the app unusable.

## Fuel economy

Economy is worked out between full tanks. Miss a fill-up and the next tank looks brilliant — it covers distance that fuel you never logged paid for. That's how a D-Max ends up claiming 21.4 km/L.

The app now spots it. A tank that reads far better than the vehicle's own history gets called out on the timeline, and opening it explains what happened and offers a single button to mark it. Marked tanks are skipped, the average goes back to the truth, and measuring starts fresh from that fill-up.

It compares against the middle of your history rather than the average, because one wild figure drags an average up and helps the next one hide.

## Accessibility

- **Sheets were invisible if you turn off animations in Windows.** The screen dimmed and nothing appeared — logging, settings, garage, search. Fixed properly: nothing now depends on an animation running to be in the right place.
- Zoom in past 160% and the panels stayed 580px wide, losing their edges off both sides. They fit the window now, and form rows wrap instead of squeezing.
- Form errors are announced and the cursor jumps to the field that stopped the save, instead of just a red border.
- The ✓ and × buttons said "check mark" and "times" to a screen reader. They say what they do now, and the remove button is big enough to hit.
- Button outlines are properly visible in both themes.
- The filter row works from the keyboard: Tab into it once, arrows to move.

## Faster

Photo thumbnails no longer load the whole picture to draw a 72-pixel tile. A phone photo used to cross into the app at full resolution every time a record was opened.

## Install

Download `Vehicle-Tracker-Setup-3.4.0.exe` and run it. Existing installs update themselves a few seconds after launch and install on quit.
