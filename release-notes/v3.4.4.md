One change: logging a fill-up is now two numbers instead of three.

**Litres work themselves out.** The price field already opens at today's published price, so now you can type just what you paid and the litres are calculated for you — total ÷ price per litre. The litres field fills in on its own with a note saying where the number came from.

If you'd rather type the litres off the pump, that still works exactly as before: litres plus price gives the total, litres plus total gives the price.

## Install

Download `Vehicle-Tracker-Setup-3.4.4.exe` and run it. Existing installs update themselves a few seconds after launch and install on quit.
